Project 01 · Health and nutrition

Nutrition Tracker and Diet Manager

A desktop application for meal logging and nutrition tracking.

Completed · Team of 3

The application lets users record meals, review calorie and macronutrient intake, and track weight measurements over time. Profile information is used to display estimated energy requirements and daily nutrition targets.

My contribution

I handled the database work using SQLite3 with Python. The database supports user profiles, food and meal entries, and weight measurements, which the application retrieves for display.

What it includes

Account creation, profile settings, a searchable food log, daily nutrition summaries, and a progress view.

Project 02 · Multivariable calculus

Electric Potential and Field Simulator

A numerical visualization project developed in GNU Octave.

Completed · Team of 3

Our team built a simulator to explore electric potential and electric field around a charge configuration. The program samples a grid, computes potential values, estimates the field from the negative gradient of potential, and visualizes the results.

Methods explored

We examined numerical estimates of the gradient and Laplacian, line integrals along multiple paths, and electric flux over spherical surfaces. Console checks compare calculations with expected relationships, including line-integral and Gauss’s-law comparisons.

How we worked

The code and presentation were developed collaboratively; team members reviewed and refined one another’s work.
